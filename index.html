The product in development

Your craft has a home.
Your business should, too.

Inked is currently in development. Our first focus is helping independent tattoo artists organize their work and business, even when their clients do not use Inked.

01

Client Management

Client contacts, notes and tattoo history, connected to projects and appointments.

02

Appointments

An artist calendar with appointment status, linked clients and projects, notes and reminders.

03

Projects

A dedicated place for tattoo projects and their connections to clients and appointments.

04

Artist Portfolio

A professional profile and portfolio that bring your work and artistic identity together.

05

Finance

Lightweight tracking of income, expenses, categories and recurring costs, with useful period summaries.

06

Business Analytics

A clearer view of recorded income and expenses, appointment activity, monthly trends and categories.

Planned

Inventory

Practical stock tracking for pigments, needles, cartridges and everyday supplies, with minimum quantities and configurable checks.

The longer-term vision

A wider vision
for tattoo life.

After the foundations for independent artists, the roadmap looks toward studio collaboration, invited client experiences and local discovery. These are future directions, with no announced release dates.

Later roadmap

Studio Operations

Shared workspaces for multiple artists, with Owner, Admin and Artist roles, shared schedules and clear access permissions. Personal finances and private business data stay separate unless access is granted.

Future direction

Digital Consent

Exploring digital consent workflows designed for tattoo professionals.

Later roadmap

Client Experience

Invited client access connected to the artist, project and appointment, with artist–client chat, artist updates and verified reviews where feasible.

Later roadmap

Local Discovery & Marketplace

Discovery by tattoo style and location, a studio map and walk-in events. Planned to grow where there is enough local artist and studio activity.
